Open-front picking bin 145 x 100 x 80 mm
PB4

The smallest open-front picking bin in the Israeli range, the shelf-mounted parts
bin used for screws, fittings and electronic components. The front wall stands at
roughly 40% of the back wall's height with the side walls sweeping down between
them on a curve, so an operator can reach in without lifting the bin off the
shelf.

The body is tapered, which lets empty bins nest into one another, and the base
sits on an inset plinth so a loaded bin stacks squarely on the one below. A rolled
lip runs around the front opening.

Open-front picking bins are not governed by a dimensional standard - sizes are
manufacturer-specific. This is the fourth and smallest size, alongside PB1 (450 x
300 x 210 mm), PB2 (295 x 205 x 160 mm) and PB3 (200 x 145 x 130 mm); dimensions
are from madafim.co.il, which prints H x W x D in cm.

STANDARD
  No dimensional standard governs this family; sizes are manufacturer-specific. These three are typical of the commonly stocked small/medium/large range.

TYPICAL USE
Built as a set-dressing and layout asset for warehouses, stockrooms, workshops and
factory scenes: pick-face and shelf fills, small-parts storage walls, WMS and
inventory-system visualisation, facility and architectural walkthroughs, training
and simulation environments, and product configurators.

Modelled at true real-world scale with clean low-poly topology and a single flat
base colour, so it instances cheaply in a wall of several hundred bins and can be
recoloured without touching the mesh.
