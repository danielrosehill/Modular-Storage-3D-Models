Open-front picking bin 200 x 145 x 130 mm
PB3

A small open-front picking bin, the shelf-mounted parts bin found on pick faces,
workshop racking and line-side supply trolleys everywhere. The front wall stands
at roughly 40% of the back wall's height with the side walls sweeping down between
them on a curve, so an operator can reach in and pick without lifting the bin off
the shelf.

The body is tapered, which lets empty bins nest into one another for storage and
transport, and the base sits on an inset plinth so a loaded bin stacks squarely on
the one below instead of sliding. A rolled lip runs around the front opening.

Unlike the euro container family, open-front picking bins are not governed by a
dimensional standard - sizes are manufacturer-specific. This is the small member
of a typical stocked small/medium/large range, and the same moulding is available
as PB1 (450 x 300 x 210 mm) and PB2 (295 x 205 x 160 mm).

TYPICAL USE
Built as a set-dressing and layout asset for warehouses, stockrooms, workshops,
maintenance stores and factory scenes: pick-face and shelf fills, small-parts
storage walls, WMS and inventory-system bin visualisation, facility and
architectural walkthroughs, training and simulation environments, and product
configurators.

Modelled at true real-world scale with clean low-poly topology and a single flat
base colour, so a wall of several hundred bins stays cheap to render and can be
recoloured per bin class without touching the mesh.
