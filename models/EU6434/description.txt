Euro stacking container 600 x 400 x 340 mm
EU6434

A solid-walled euro stacking container on the 600 x 400 mm footprint, in the deep
340 mm height. The footprint is the standard European small load carrier module,
four of which fill a 1200 x 800 mm Euro pallet layer exactly; the extra height
makes this the bulk-storage member of the family, used where the 230 mm crate runs
out of volume before it runs out of weight.

The model carries the features that define the container's silhouette and how it
behaves in a scene: straight external walls with rounded rim and corner radii,
hand-hole grips cut through the top rail on both long sides, a hollowed underside,
and a recessed stacking foot that drops inside the rim of the container below, so
a stack interlocks rather than sliding.

Fine surface detail - the moulded rib columns on the walls and the waffle grid
under the base - is deliberately left out. It is not readable at the scales this
asset is built for, and it would multiply the triangle count for no visible gain.

Open top. Lids are a separate item on the real product and are not modelled here.

DIMENSIONS (external)
  Length    600 mm
  Width     400 mm
  Height    340 mm

STANDARD
  600 x 400 mm footprint: the standard euro container module, four per 1200 x 800 mm Euro pallet layer. Same grid as the VDA 4500 small load carrier family.

TYPICAL USE
Built as a set-dressing and layout asset for industrial storage, warehousing,
logistics and manufacturing scenes: shelf and rack fills, pallet builds, WMS and
inventory-system bin visualisation, warehouse layout planning, facility and
architectural walkthroughs, training and simulation environments, and product
configurators.

Modelled at true real-world scale with clean low-poly topology and a single flat
base colour, so it instances cheaply hundreds of times in a shelf or rack view and
can be recoloured per bin type without touching the mesh.
