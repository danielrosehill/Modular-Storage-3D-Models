== body ==
An attached-lid container (ALC), also called a distribution tote or a swan-neck
crate, on the standard 600 x 400 mm euro footprint - four per 1200 x 800 mm Euro
pallet layer. ALCs are the workhorse of retail distribution, returnable transit
packaging and internal transfer, because the lid travels with the box and cannot
be lost.

The geometry differs from a straight-wall euro crate in ways that matter:

- An integral two-part hinged lid that meets on the centre line, with
  interlocking tabs along the join and hasp tabs at the front edge for a security
  seal.
- A tapered body with horizontal rib bands, which is what lets these nest inside
  one another when empty with the lid open, and stack squarely when closed and
  loaded. That taper means an ALC is not a scaled-up euro crate.

The lid is modelled closed and fused to the body.

== use ==
Built as a set-dressing and layout asset for industrial storage, warehousing,
logistics and distribution scenes: pallet and roll-cage builds, returnable
packaging flows, WMS and inventory-system visualisation, warehouse layout
planning, facility and architectural walkthroughs, training and simulation
environments, and product configurators.

Modelled at true real-world scale with clean low-poly topology and a single flat
base colour, so it instances cheaply across a pallet or aisle view and can be
recoloured without touching the mesh.
