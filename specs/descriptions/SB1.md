== summary ==
Boltless warehouse shelving bay, 1600 x 600 x 2300 mm - true-scale GLB of a five-level steel racking bay sized to the euro container module.

== body ==
A boltless (rivet) steel shelving bay of the kind used throughout warehouses,
stockrooms, archives and workshops: four corner posts, front and back beams at
each level, shelf decks, and diagonal bracing on the side frames. Five levels.

The bay is dimensioned around the euro container module, which is what makes it
useful as a layout asset rather than generic racking: the 600 mm depth matches the
long side of a 600 x 400 mm euro container exactly, and the 1600 mm width takes
four 400 mm-wide containers per level - twenty per bay across five levels. Drop
the euro containers from this same set onto it and the fit is correct by
construction, not by eye.

Shelf pitch is a representative spacing rather than a manufacturer's figure.

== use ==
Built as a set-dressing and layout asset for warehouse, stockroom, archive,
workshop and factory scenes: aisle and rack runs, storage capacity planning, WMS
and inventory-system location visualisation, facility and architectural
walkthroughs, training and simulation environments.

Modelled at true real-world scale with clean low-poly topology and a single flat
material, so a full aisle of bays stays cheap to render and instances without
rescaling.
