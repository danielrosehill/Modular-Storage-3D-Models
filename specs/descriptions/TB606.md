== summary ==
Tilt-out bin unit, 6 bins, 91 x 600 x 113 mm - true-scale GLB of a 600 mm wide multi-cell tilt-bin housing for shelving and parts storage.

== body ==
A tilt-out bin unit (Hebrew: רב-תא, "multi-cell") with 6 bins side by side: a
fixed open-front housing in which each bin pivots forward on its bottom edge, so
small parts can be seen and reached without removing anything. This is the 6-bin
member of a range that shares one 600 mm (23 5/8 in) width, so units of different
bin counts stack into a single wall; only height and depth change.

The same moulding is sold in the US as Akro-Mils TiltView 06706, with identical
inch figures, so there is no separate US model. Dimensions are confirmed from the
manufacturer's carton and cross-checked against madafim.co.il, within about 2 mm.

All 6 bins are modelled closed.

== use ==
Built as a set-dressing and layout asset for workshops, stockrooms, maintenance
stores and electronics or hardware storage scenes: wall-mounted and shelf-top bin
walls, small-parts and fastener storage, WMS and inventory-system visualisation,
facility and architectural walkthroughs, training and simulation environments, and
product configurators.

Modelled at true real-world scale with clean low-poly topology and a single flat
base colour, so it instances cheaply across a wall of several units and can be
recoloured without touching the mesh.
