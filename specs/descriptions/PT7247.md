== body ==
A folding platform truck (flatbed trolley) of the common 150 kg class: a 720 x 470 mm steel deck with a rubber mat, four 100 mm casters, and a tubular U handle that folds flat over the deck for storage. Overall 730 x 470 x 820 mm with the handle up. One 600 x 400 euro container fits on the deck with room to spare.

Unbranded on purpose: the crossbar panel is blank and the colours are neutral. Dimensions are from a retailer specification; the deck height (150 mm) and hinge details are inferred from product photos. The folded state is published as PT7247F.

== use ==
Built for storage and moving planning: checking what fits on the deck, how a stack of euro containers moves between rooms, and where the trolley is stored when folded. Also usable as a set-dressing asset for warehouse, workshop and home-move scenes.

Modelled at true real-world scale with low-poly geometry and flat materials (grey frame, black mat, dark tyres).
