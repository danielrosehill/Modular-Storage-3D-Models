== body ==
A US attached-lid container (ALC), also called a distribution tote, 27 x 17 x 12.5
in (686 x 432 x 318 mm), modelled on Akro-Mils 39160 from Akro-Mils sell sheet
AKM809. The lid travels with the box and cannot be lost, which is why ALCs are the
workhorse of retail distribution, returnable transit packaging and internal
transfer in the US.

The geometry differs from a straight-wall tote in ways that matter:

- An integral two-part hinged lid that meets on the centre line, with interlocking
  tabs along the join and hasp tabs at the front edge for a security seal.
- A tapered body with horizontal rib bands, which lets these nest when empty with
  the lid open and stack squarely when closed and loaded.

This is the larger 27 x 17 in footprint of the US range, longer than the 21.5 x
15.25 in distribution size and than the 600 x 400 euro module. The lid is modelled
closed and fused to the body.

== use ==
Built as a set-dressing and layout asset for US distribution, retail logistics and
returnable packaging: pallet and roll-cage builds, returnable packaging flows, WMS
and inventory-system visualisation, facility and architectural walkthroughs,
training and simulation environments, and product configurators.

Modelled at true real-world scale with clean low-poly topology and a single flat
base colour, so it instances cheaply across a pallet or aisle view and can be
recoloured without touching the mesh.
