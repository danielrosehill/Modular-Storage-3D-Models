== body ==
A US straight-wall stacking container, 23.75 x 15.75 x 8.25 in (603 x 400 x 210
mm), modelled on the Akro-Mils 37688 solid-wall tote from the Akro-Mils straight-
wall catalogue. Nominal 24 x 16 in, which in practice is the 600 x 400 mm euro
module, so it sits on the same pallet pattern as a euro crate.

It shares the straight-wall builder with the euro containers: straight external
walls, rounded rim and corner radii, hand-hole grips through the top rail on the
long sides, a hollowed underside and a recessed stacking foot that drops inside
the rim of the container below. Inch dimensions are confirmed from the
manufacturer's listing and converted to mm.

Open top. Lids are a separate item on the real product and are not modelled here.

== use ==
Built as a set-dressing and layout asset for US-style storage, warehousing,
logistics and manufacturing: shelf and rack fills, pallet builds, WMS and
inventory-system visualisation, facility and architectural walkthroughs, training
and simulation environments, and product configurators.

Modelled at true real-world scale with clean low-poly topology and a single flat
base colour, so it instances cheaply hundreds of times in a shelf or rack view and
can be recoloured without touching the mesh.
